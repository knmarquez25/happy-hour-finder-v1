## What This Sample Establishes

- Three branch pages provide clear happy-hour schedules: both Wood Ranch branches and Lazy Dog Irvine. This is source evidence, not same-day confirmation or a complete priced offer record.

- Chain discovery needs branch-specific review. Locality, restaurant address versus parking address, source age, source domain ownership, exceptions, and late-night hours all create distinct research tasks.
