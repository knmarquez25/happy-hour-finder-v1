## Method and limits

150 existing official-menu/location URLs were checked plus newly located official DTLA sources. HTML collection checked robots/access policy and did not bypass denials. Readable HH sections were compared with saved records; regular menu sections were excluded. Eleven accessible PDF/image sources were downloaded temporarily; relevant pages were rendered and visually reviewed, including Casa Martin, Simonette, Ospi, Lunetta, Library Alehouse, Brookside, Gracias Madre, L&E and Lawry's. Brookside's PDF has regular menus only. Failed/blocked/dynamic-menu access and unresolved daily-offer scopes are recorded individually in the ledger/report. Fetched text alone did not establish completeness.

Two complete outcomes are the 33 Taps branches; other expanded records remain partial where daily-offer scope, price, schedule or menu access remains unresolved. Legacy samples at sources that could not be rechecked are preserved with their original evidence dates, not relabeled as current confirmation. No owner approvals, background refreshes, paid discovery calls, remote publishing or deployment were performed.
